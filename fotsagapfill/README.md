# FotSA Gap-Fill (fotsagapfill)

A compat / gap-fill add-on for Fauna of the Stone Age (FotSA) by Tenth Architect. This is NOT FotSA and does not replace it.

It adds missing Stone Age fauna as variants of installed FotSA packs (plus one own-domain entity derived from the vanilla bear). It requires the donor FotSA packs to be installed; the entity and shape JSON here is generated from them by the scripts in tools/.

Credit: FotSA and its models and art belong to Tenth Architect and the FotSA authors. Please see their pages for their terms.

Contents: modinfo.json and assets/ (generated JSON: entities, shapes, lang). The texture PNGs from the packaged 0.2.0 build are intentionally NOT included, since they derive from FotSA art. tools/ holds the generator scripts (they expect local copies of the donor packs). NOTES-REPORT.md is the build report.

Version 0.2.0. Not on ModDB.
